# Cort3x

> **Innovation Intelligence OS**

[Cort3x](https://cort3x.app) is an operating system for structured innovation and strategic opportunity discovery. It connects research, market signals, opportunity mapping, decision frameworks and execution into one persistent intelligence workflow.

<p align="center"><strong>Research → Signal → Opportunity → Decision → Execution</strong></p>

---

## Why it exists

Innovation work is often fragmented across search tabs, presentations, notes, spreadsheets and one-off AI conversations. The result is difficult to compare, difficult to govern and almost impossible to trace back to evidence.

Cort3x turns that process into a system.

It is designed to preserve the chain between:

- source evidence;
- market or technology signals;
- hypotheses;
- opportunity assessment;
- strategic decisions;
- actions and follow-up.

## Core capabilities

- structured research and evidence capture;
- market and technology intelligence;
- opportunity mapping;
- comparative assessment;
- strategic frameworks and prioritization;
- AI-assisted synthesis and explanation;
- persistent decision context;
- execution planning and follow-up;
- traceability from recommendation back to source evidence.

---

## Product principles

1. **Evidence before narrative.** A compelling story is not a substitute for source quality.
2. **Research should compound.** Useful findings become reusable organizational intelligence.
3. **AI assists judgment.** It can structure, compare and explain; it does not silently become the authority.
4. **Opportunities need explicit assumptions.** Uncertainty stays visible.
5. **Decisions should lead to action.** Intelligence without an execution path is incomplete.
6. **History matters.** The system should preserve why a decision was made and what evidence supported it.

---

## Architecture

Cort3x is implemented as a modern TypeScript web application with persistent data, structured workflows and AI-assisted intelligence surfaces.

Deployment credentials, private research material and client-specific evidence are intentionally excluded from public repository documentation.
